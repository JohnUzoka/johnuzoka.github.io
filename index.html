Bio

John Uzoka is a video game programmer specializing in C++, Unreal Engine, and Godot Engine. He has experience developing gameplay mechanics, optimizing systems, and integrating assets in ways that balance technical performance with artistic intent. He also works in UI/UX design, focusing on clarity and player-centered interaction.

Artist Statement

I develop games centered on meaningful interaction, using mechanics and UI as artistic tools to support emotion and narrative. Inspired by retro games, I value minimalism and technical optimization, embracing constraint as a creative force that encourages clarity, intentional design, and strong visual identity. I focus on engaging players through distinct, evolving challenges that promote learning, reflection, and agency. My goal is to craft expressive, carefully refined experiences that stay with players long after the controller is set down.
