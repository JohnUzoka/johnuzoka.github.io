Portfolio

A selection of recent work and experiments. These entries are placeholders for now—each will link out to details later.

Deep Diver

A 2D platformer made in Godot engine with different types of enemies where you dive deeper through randomized levels, gaining more loot the deeper you go, but also more rewards.

Crypt Raider

A first-person puzzle game made in Unreal Engine with C++ where you invade a space ship and embark through it searching for keycards to open doors and steal the treasure.

Toon Tanks

A 2D top-down tank game made in Unreal Engine with C++ featuring a cartoonish art style and challenging Enemy AI.

ThirdPerson Shooter

A third-person shooter game made in Unreal Engine as a one-day challenge, featuring different weapons, third-person movement and aiming, as well as health reduction and regeneration.

Obstacle Assault

A fast-paced 2D platformer game made in Unreal Engine with C++ where you navigate through challenging obstacle courses with varying difficulty levels.

DreamScape

A 2D game made in Bisy, where you work as a dream detective solving puzzles in surreal environments.

Warehouse Wreckage

A first-person game made in Unreal Engine, where you have a limited amount of ammo and the goal is to cause as much damage in a warehouse, highlighting the game’s physics system.

Man vs Bot

A fast-paced third-person shooter made in Unreal Engine with C++, where you fight to clear out robot AI enemies in a town.

Haunted House

A text-based adventure game made with C++, where you explore a haunted house and solve puzzles in order to escape without dying.

Shepherd's Crook

A 3D model made in Blender using low-poly geometry and fully procedural textures to create a stylized wooden shepherd’s crook, potentially as a video game asset.
