Space Raider

A first-person puzzle game made in Unreal Engine with C++ where you invade a space ship and embark through it searching for keycards to open doors and steal the treasure.

Unreal Engine C++ First-Person Puzzle Problem Solving Level Design

The Concept

This game is a first-person puzzle experience where you board a spaceship, search for key cards, and navigate your way through locked doors to steal the ship's power core. Simple premise, but it gave me the perfect sandbox to learn core Unreal C++ concepts.

What I Built & Learned

This was a shorter project, but it packed in a lot of foundational skills:

  • Lighting and level design - I learned how to use light to guide the player, create mood, and make the spaceship feel believable. Good lighting turned a modular asset pack into a real environment.
  • Traces and collisions - I implemented line traces for interactions (opening doors, picking up key cards) and learned how to tune collision responses so the player could interact cleanly with the world.
  • Trigger elements - Overlap volumes that unlock doors.
  • Camera shake - A small detail, but adding subtle shake during certain actions made the game feel more grounded and immersive.

Asset Workflow

I used a free modular asset pack to block out and design the ship's layout, which let me focus on level design rather than building every mesh from scratch. I also created a few of my own assets to fill gaps and add a personal touch to the environment.

Why This Matters to Me

This project wasn't about shipping a big game. It was about building strong fundamentals in Unreal C++, understanding how the pieces fit together, from input handling to collision events to level scripting. Every mechanic I built reinforced a core game development pattern that I find implementing in some way in other c++ projects.

Final Thought

Short project, solid foundation. I walked away with practical experience in Unreal C++ and a much better eye for level design and lighting. Sometimes the smaller projects teach you the most.
